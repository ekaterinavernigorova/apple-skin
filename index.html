Sustainability by Design

APPLE SKIN is produced using apple processing byproducts combined with plant-based and recycled components. This approach reduces reliance on animal resources and lowers the environmental footprint compared to traditional leather production.

Technology &Production

APPLE SKIN is developed using proprietary production technology that combines material science expertise with industrial manufacturing standards. The process is optimized for consistency, scalability, and performance, enabling reliable supply for commercial applications.

Driven by Material Innovation
Leading global brands across fashion, furniture, and automotive industries are increasingly adopting plant-based biomaterials as alternatives to animal leather.
